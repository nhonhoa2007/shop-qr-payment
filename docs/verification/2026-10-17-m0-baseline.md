   - Conservative rectangular bounding box aggregation and SHA-256 revision hash shared across fee calculation and carrier fulfillment (`tests/shipping-packing.test.ts`).
6. **High-Value Fulfillment Review & RBAC (Task 8):**
   - Orders > 5,000,000 VND gated to `REVIEW` with structured outcomes (`CANCEL_AND_REFUND`, `EXTERNAL_SHIPMENT`). Granular staff permissions added (`tests/high-value-fulfillment.test.ts`).
7. **Approved Rating Aggregation (Task 9):**
   - `CatalogService.getHomeCatalog` filters `reviews` by `where: { isApproved: true }` at the Prisma relation level; unapproved reviews excluded from `avgRating` and `reviewCount` (`tests/catalog-rating.test.ts`).
